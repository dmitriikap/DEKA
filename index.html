How we work

Five things we commit to on every job.

Managers change builders over communication far more often than over price. These are the promises you can hold us to.

01

Site attendance and quotation within five business days

A date when you call and a price when we say. If site conditions change that, you hear it before the date passes — not after.

02

A photographic scope appendix with every quotation

Marked-up site photographs showing exactly what is included and what is not, so a committee can see what it's approving and nobody argues about the extent of works later.

03

Fixed lump sums with a written exclusions schedule

No provisional sums buried in the total. Where a risk genuinely can't be priced until the ground is opened, we say so up front and tell you how it will be priced.

04

A written progress update every Friday, on every open job

What was done, what's next and anything needing a decision — whether or not there's news to report. You should never have to chase us for the status of a job.

05

A standing schedule of rates for recurring minor works

Your office raises a work order and it gets attended, without waiting on a fresh quotation every time.
